## 1. Path 1 now versus Path 2 first

### Position: split Path 1 before comparing ROI

| Path | Defensible proposition | Position now |
|---|---|---|
| 1A: workflow education/tools | A reproducible planning, journalling and review process; clearly unvalidated as a trading edge | Small, paid, fixed-scope pilot after accuracy and usability checks |
| 1B: actionable signals/performance-led course | Specific trading actions or a claim that following the strategy improves investment returns | Defer pending outcome evidence and appropriate compliance review |
| 2: direct trading | Risk capital on a demonstrated, executable edge | Paper validation only; no reliable near-term income forecast |
| 3: managed capital | Operate or advise on other people's capital | Not an urgency solution; track record and legal/operational requirements, not merely an 18-36 month clock |
| 4: prop evaluation | Earn contractual rewards after meeting a particular programme's rules | Research and rule-matched simulation; paid attempt gated |
| 5: productised service | Deliver a useful service using skills already demonstrated | Best candidate for near-term cash, conditional on actual buyers |

Within V2, 1A offers the best *bounded experiment*: a small build, reversible expenditure and no need to wager capital to discover willingness to pay. That is a qualitative risk-adjusted judgement, not a calculable expected ROI. Acquisition cost, conversion, retention, support hours and buyers' willingness to pay are unknown. Path 5 may beat every trading-related option on time to cash if there are existing clients and credible skills.

Reject the assertion that the method is better than 90% of retail content: no comparative study supports it. Key levels, stops, event awareness and R:R are useful organising concepts, but are not an established edge or distinctive product by themselves. A sound-looking process can consistently select losing trades.

Also reject the claim that one bad month inevitably destroys a brand. Losses are normal even for some profitable strategies. Undisclosed risk, cherry-picking and promises incompatible with the evidence are the more avoidable reputational problems.

### Minimum viable evidence depends on the promise

| Offer | Minimum defensible evidence before offering it |
|---|---|
| Free retrospective learning journal | Accurate worked examples; dated versions; failures and limitations included; no suggestion of verified profitability |
| Paid workflow pilot | Independently checked examples and calculations; explicit deliverables; users can reproduce the workflow; clear unvalidated-strategy disclosure; terms and support scope |
| Recurring research membership | The above, plus demonstrated repeat value, a sustainable publishing cadence and genuine renewal demand |
| Actionable signal/performance product | Prospective, complete and resolved outcomes; realistic execution and costs; leakage-controlled out-of-sample validation; uncertainty and drawdown reporting; legal review of actual content and marketing |

For 1A, propose 3-5 independent pilot users and seek 3-5 genuine paid commitments before building a broad course/community. These are economical product-discovery gates, **not statistical proof or an industry standard**. Measure whether customers can complete a brief/review correctly, time saved, support required, and willingness to renew or recommend.

For 1B and Path 2, neither 30 outcomes, 50 briefs, 90 calendar days nor PF >= 1.10 is an automatic pass. Briefs are not independent trades; multiple signals can express the same exposure. Sample size must reflect the size and variability of the claimed edge. Examine net expectancy, drawdown, exposure, sample dependence, parameter selection and sensitivity to worse costs. Freeze the rule set before evaluating a genuinely unseen period; do not repeatedly optimise on the supposed holdout. A small PF margin can disappear under modest execution error.

A few service customers can be commercially useful long before that audience exists.

## 2. What the 2026 market actually supports

### Challenge to Claude's market assumptions

| Claim | Research-based position |

| Prop firms dominate aspiring-trader monetisation | A substantial visible category, but no representative market-share evidence found that establishes dominance across education, tools, signals and trading |
| Props remove personal-capital risk | They reduce the need to supply the advertised account balance; fees, repeated attempts, infrastructure, time and non-payment/contract risks remain |

There are specific, current developments worth using instead of extrapolation:

- **Trading workflow software is direct competition.** TradeZella's July 13, 2026 announcement, effective July 14, lists monthly plans at USD 35, 59 and 99, with combinations of journalling, review reports, replay, backtesting and AI features. This places a GBP 29-49 framework subscription against substantial existing functionality, not only low-quality signal groups. Price is evidence of an offer, not its conversion rate. [TradeZella pricing announcement](https://help.tradezella.com/en/articles/8911582-our-pricing)
- **Systematic paid research is an existing category.** Quantifiable Edges sells subscription research; Edgewonk sells trading-journal analysis. Free learning resources also compete for introductory attention. These are useful category comparators, not proof of demand for V2's exact approach. [Quantifiable Edges](https://quantifiableedges.com/), [Edgewonk](https://edgewonk.com/pricing), [BabyPips learning resources](https://www.babypips.com/learn)
- **Prop consolidation is real, but not a market-share denominator.** FTMO/OANDA announcements record acquisition completion in December 2025 and the conclusion of OANDA Prop Trader in March 2026. That supports an inference of consolidation and changing distribution, not guaranteed educator earnings. [FTMO/OANDA press releases](https://ftmo.oanda.com/press-releases/)

V2 maps most naturally to a **pre-trade planning and post-trade review workflow**, potentially with a focused teaching service. Its plausible differentiation is a narrow audience, reproducible examples, honest no-trade decisions, useful review and low-friction delivery. Whether that is valuable enough to buy remains a hypothesis. Do not build a generic journal platform to compete with established suites before discovering a specific unmet need.

### Affiliates: possible later, with conflicts made explicit

FTMO currently advertises CFD affiliate commissions of 8-20% on a referred customer's first challenge purchase. Its futures programme has a different recurring structure. The5ers also has a referral programme aimed at educators and other affiliates. These establish a monetisation mechanism, not a dependable income level. [FTMO affiliate programme](https://ftmo.com/en/affiliate-programme/), [The5ers referral programme](https://the5ers.com/refer-a-trader/)

Recommendation: leave affiliate income out of the launch base case. It creates an incentive to recommend paid attempts even when the audience should practise for free. If introduced later, disclose the relationship prominently, separate editorial judgement from payout, and never make challenge purchase a prerequisite for learning. UK advertising rules require affiliate advertising to be identifiable. [ASA affiliate-marketing guidance](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/affiliate-marketing.html)

### Compliance is a product-design constraint

Calling material education or adding a disclaimer does not settle whether its substance is a financial promotion or regulated activity. A public or paid channel is not a general exemption. Obtain a UK perimeter/financial-promotions review before publishing specific actionable recommendations, personalised guidance or investment-related affiliate promotions; the precise instrument, communication and business model matter. Not all general education is regulated. [FCA financial promotions on social media, FG24/1](https://www.fca.org.uk/publication/finalised-guidance/fg24-1.pdf)

Separately, earnings claims for courses must be substantiated; exceptional success stories are not typical customer outcomes. Do not imply that this workflow replaces a salary, reliably passes evaluations or delivers a stated return. [ASA: money-making courses](https://www.asa.org.uk/news/money-making-courses-stick-to-the-facts.html)

### Economics and gate

An advertised GBP 100,000 account is not GBP 100,000 of owned capital. A 10% maximum-loss allowance is only GBP 10,000 of permitted loss before other restrictions. A hypothetical 10% annual return at an assumed 80% reward share produces GBP 8,000 before fees and tax, **conditional on passing, retaining eligibility and receiving payment**. It is not expected income.

The useful model is:

`expected receipts = P(pass) * P(eligible payout | pass) * expected conditional rewards - fees - infrastructure - other costs`

Opportunity cost also matters. No reliable V2 estimates exist for those probabilities; therefore the prompt's allocation-times-return calculation is not an ROI forecast. Challenge passes are selected outcomes, not sufficient evidence of repeatable long-term alpha.

Before any paid attempt: repair accounting/execution gaps; freeze and validate the strategy; simulate the exact selected contract at account level; test intrabar loss breaches, resets, news, restarts and simultaneous exposure; reconcile broker statements; obtain terms clarification; and set one affordable, predetermined fee budget with no automatic retakes. Free trial/rule-matched paper work comes first. Passing one short demo window is not sufficient on its own.

## Addendum: what the MT5 feed actually changes

### Useful advance, narrower than claimed

The runner can turn broker-feed bars into prospective timestamped strategy observations. That helps test data availability and whether signals would have existed before outcomes were known. It does **not** currently connect `Signal -> PaperBroker -> fills -> positions -> exits -> account ledger`.

Code-grounded limitations:

1. `scripts/run_mt5_live_paper.py:111` generates a signal from the last completed bar and logs BUY/SELL only. There is no simulated fill, position lifecycle, realised outcome or account-level loss enforcement.
2. ATR at line 81 uses exponential smoothing, while `src/tar_system/features/engineering.py:40` uses a rolling mean. Resolve feature parity before treating feed results and backtests as the same experiment.
3. Line 116 hard-codes `NEUTRAL`. This does not prospectively validate the full regime workflow described in the commercial proposition.
4. There is no durable processed-bar cursor or restart-safe deduplication, and no replay of missed bars after downtime. In-memory strategy cooldown is not persistent exactly-once processing.
5. A historical bar-close entry observed on a 60-second polling loop is not proof of an executable fill at that price. Store observation time, bid/ask, spread, latency and subsequent execution assumptions.
6. Data checks reject an empty fetch but do not establish sufficient history, freshness or expected symbol/account identity. Rejected/no-trade decisions and feed outages need an audit trail too.
7. The advertised `data/live_signals/` directory was absent at inspection. This does not prove the script has never run elsewhere, but there was no saved run evidence at its stated output location.

The sealed live modules are interfaces, not completed execution infrastructure. Flipping a flag is not a substitute for idempotent orders, reconciliation, rejection handling, partial-fill handling, risk enforcement, recovery and fail-closed behaviour. The current script contains no order placement; its safety must not be extrapolated to a future live adapter. Treat the addendum's hour estimates as rough prototype estimates only.

There is also a deployment prerequisite: the current official MetaTrader5 Python package publishes Windows x86-64 wheels, not native macOS wheels. On the local Mac, a normal native `pip install` is not the whole solution. A supported Windows environment, or a separately verified compatibility arrangement, introduces work and possibly hosting cost. No such installation was attempted. [MetaQuotes package releases and files](https://pypi.org/project/metatrader5/)

A CCXT exchange-crypto adapter is not automatically useful for a prop firm's BTCUSD instrument. Exchange crypto and a broker's CFD can have different contracts, spreads, sessions, sizing and execution. Use the selected venue's actual instrument and avoid introducing a second broker architecture before it is needed.

### Are 12 briefs plus paper signals sufficient?

**For illustrating and piloting the workflow: potentially yes, after checking accuracy and describing them honestly. For selling an edge, live alerts or prop readiness: no.** Twelve dated briefs establish a small collection of examples, not realised performance. A date in a local filename is not independently verified proof of pre-market publication. Prospective records need immutable versions or equivalent timestamp evidence, linked outcomes and disclosure of later amendments.

Even a much larger collection of entry-only signals cannot establish net returns. Preserve each original brief, strategy/configuration version, feed source, eligible session, every decision, proposed entry, assumed executable fill, costs, exit and outcome. Distinguish backtest, prospective simulation and actual execution. Do not cherry-pick winning signals or omit no-trade days. Public claims require additional care and explicit permission to release any private material.

**Prop calculus verdict:** the feed lowers future observation friction; it does not yet improve an estimated pass probability, remove a validation gate or justify paying for an evaluation.

## 4. Best 3-6 month sequence

Choose a modified Option A, with Option B's evidence discipline and a separate near-term cash route. Neither a calendar deadline nor financial urgency creates strategy readiness.

### Weeks 1-2: cash discovery and reliable evidence

- Privately establish the minimum required income and affordable experiment budget; assume zero trading/prop income in the essential-spending plan.
- Offer one service grounded in existing demonstrable ability: documentation, journal/report setup, data-quality review or operational workflow implementation. Exclude trade recommendations, managing accounts and challenge-passing services.
- Hold 10-15 relevant buyer conversations. Establish the painful task, current alternative and willingness to pay before expanding the product.
- Prepare one concise retrospective workflow sample; no profit promise, live alerts or compulsory affiliate purchase.
- Prioritise canonical feed features, durable decisions/outcomes, current cost-model regression coverage, sizing/accounting and continuity. Keep trading controls unchanged.

### Weeks 3-6: a narrow paid pilot

- Seek 3-5 paying workflow/service customers with a defined deliverable and acceptance criterion. Avoid building a large course, app or always-on Discord first.
- Measure cash received, delivery hours, support load, refund requests and customer usefulness. These are the commercial outcomes missing from the original debate.
- Continue frozen-rule prospective paper collection, including losses, rejected trades and outages. Fix critical defects openly and start a new labelled experiment version where necessary.
- If roughly 20 well-targeted conversations produce no credible paid commitments, revise the problem/audience/offer rather than assume that more features will solve demand. This is a proposed stop rule, not a market benchmark.

### Weeks 7-12: repeat what customers actually value

- Only introduce a recurring research/workflow membership if recurring value and willingness to renew are demonstrated. GBP 29-49 is a price hypothesis to test, not a pre-approved launch price.
- Keep service work as the nearer-term income route where it converts. Do not split attention across a course, newsletter, Discord, two live adapters and affiliates simultaneously.
- Review a regenerated, leakage-controlled validation report with uncertainty and drawdown, not PF alone. Extend the observation period if evidence is weak or trade frequency low.
- Model one prop programme in paper mode if the ledger and risk controls are trustworthy; otherwise continue repairing evidence collection.

### Months 4-6: conditional expansion, not forced launch

- Expand the workflow offer only on actual retention and delivery economics.
- Consider a capped paid prop attempt only if all engineering, performance, contract and affordability gates pass, with separate human approval. Otherwise remain on paper/free trial.
- Consider actionable signals or direct capital only after the stronger evidence and compliance gates; neither becomes acceptable automatically in month six.
- Leave managed capital outside this urgent plan. A credible history alone does not resolve permissions, custody, client obligations or operations.

### Risks the original options underweight

Distribution and support costs; subscription churn; tax/refund obligations; sample dependence and selection bias; deadline-driven overfitting; data/brief rights; customer privacy; feed/OS deployment effort; prop contract changes and payout constraints; account-wide rather than per-trade drawdown; affiliate conflicts; and the conflict between public copyable signals and some firms' prohibited practices.

Process transparency and results transparency are not substitutes. A workflow customer needs proof that the workflow is useful; a performance customer needs the complete, appropriately qualified outcome record. Neither type of transparency by itself proves demand.
