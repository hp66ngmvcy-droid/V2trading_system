## Eight improvements to debate

1. **Freeze one baseline strategy.** Record signal, entry, stop, target, horizon, session, cost and missing-data rules with code/config/data hashes. Keep zone-touch and wick-rejection models separately named. Change one experimental component at a time so differences remain attributable.

2. **Collect prospective paper observations.** Commit timestamped inputs and decisions before outcomes unfold. Later corrections must be linked revisions, not rewrites of the original decision. Use a 10-20 trading-day pilot to test collection reliability and identify hypotheses, not as an automatic profitability or statistical-significance gate.

3. **Measure missed opportunities.** Preserve the common candidate universe and the reason every candidate was accepted, rejected or left unresolved. Evaluate hypothetical outcomes for rejected candidates using the same frozen execution assumptions, explicitly labelled counterfactual. Report coverage, exposure and net contribution as well as win rate.

4. **Compare against simple controls.** Use an ungated mechanical baseline to assess incremental value from trend, human judgement or AI classification. Hold data availability, candidate selection, fills, exits and costs constant. Human and machine arms remain separate; machine labels cannot substitute for missing human evidence. No external model execution is authorised by this proposal.

5. **Separate BTC and gold conclusions.** Report instrument-specific results, data quality and session conventions. A pooled result must not conceal opposite effects. Keep session/regime subgroup analysis exploratory until sufficient evidence exists; avoid creating many small cells and choosing the best afterwards.

6. **Stress-test execution assumptions.** Predeclare plausible spread/slippage, delayed-entry, gap and missing-data scenarios. Identify the costs at which apparent expectancy disappears. Separate measured execution evidence from assumed stress scenarios. No live execution is needed.

7. **Record decision reasons.** Save concise rule outcomes, evidence references, availability timestamps and a short decision rationale for BUY/SELL/WAIT. Record which rule failed, not merely the final label. This means an auditable decision summary, not private model chain-of-thought. Reuse the existing reviewer/outcome contracts where possible.

8. **Define stopping and revision rules.** Set evaluation dates and conditions for stopping, deferring or revising an idea before examining results. Distinguish invalid data/implementation failures from poor strategy performance and insufficient evidence. Log every attempted variant; a revised hypothesis starts a new version rather than silently replacing a failed result. Preserve genuinely unexamined/prospective evidence for final evaluation.
